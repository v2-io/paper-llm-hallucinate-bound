## Conclusion ^sec-conclusion

The opening question — *how much can an LLM be pulled away from its evidence by its goal?* — sits on an axis the frequency lineage doesn't engage. A coupled-architecture model can simultaneously have an inevitable-frequency hallucination floor (training-data structure, calibration, capacity) *and* a bounded-magnitude goal-coupling displacement on each event — orthogonal axes, complementary readings.

**Limitations.** Conditional theorems under named regularity ((H2$'$), (H4$'$), (H$_\kappa$)) and the (PI) axiomatic commitment, on theory-only grounds — one channel of hallucination, complementary to the frequency lineage.

**Future directions.** *First instantiation of $\hat\kappa_{\text{processing}}$ on a deployed LLM*: the bound becomes operational once $\kappa^*$ is bounded; binary-uniform two-goal probing has a closed-form JSD estimator ([[#^thm-umbrella]]), with activation-level mediation via representation-engineering / steering-vector / mechanistic-interpretability for the architectural side. *Sycophancy* \citep{sharma-2023-sycophancy} — LLM responses matching user beliefs over truthful ones across state-of-the-art assistants — is the natural empirical referent; Sharma et al.'s *answer sycophancy metric* is essentially the binary-uniform two-goal probe of [[#^cor-architectural-factorization]], so instantiating the bound on existing sycophancy benchmarks would convert them into magnitude-bound diagnostics. *Bridging to the frequency-lower-bound lineage*: a layered theory composing Kalai-Vempala-style frequency-floors with the goal-coupling magnitude-ceiling here would give the field a complete two-axis picture.
