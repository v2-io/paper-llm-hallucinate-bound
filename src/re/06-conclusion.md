## Conclusion ^sec-conclusion

The opening question — *how much can an LLM be pulled away from what its evidence supports by the goal it is asked to satisfy?* — sits on an axis the existing hallucination-theory lineage doesn't engage. The lineage bounds *frequency*; the question is about *size*. The two axes are orthogonal: a coupled-architecture model in (PI)-compatible operating regime can simultaneously have an inevitable-frequency hallucination floor (training-data structure, calibration, capacity) *and* a bounded-magnitude goal-coupling displacement on each event (geometric structure).

**Limitations.** The bounds are conditional theorems. Regularity hypotheses (H2$'$) and (H4$'$) restrict the regime each Track applies in; (PI) is an axiomatic commitment, not a derivation; the architectural factorization requires (H$_\kappa$) at corollary level. The paper is theory-only — we do not instrument a deployed LLM's belief-update dynamics. The bound captures *one channel* of hallucination — goal-coupling-induced displacement — which is real and structurally derived; other channels (training-data structure, calibration, capacity) live in the orthogonal frequency lineage.

**Future directions.** Two are highest-leverage. *First instantiation of $\hat\kappa_{\text{processing}}$ on a deployed LLM*: the bound becomes operational once $\kappa^*$ is bounded; binary-uniform two-goal probing has a closed-form JSD estimator ([[#^thm-umbrella]]'s empirical-accessibility remark), and activation-level mediation via representation-engineering / steering-vector / mechanistic-interpretability lines gives a tighter handle. *Sycophancy* \citep{sharma-2023-sycophancy} — LLM responses matching user beliefs over truthful ones across state-of-the-art assistants — is the natural empirical referent; Sharma et al.'s *answer sycophancy metric* is essentially the binary-uniform two-goal probe of [[#^cor-architectural-factorization]], so instantiating the bound on existing sycophancy benchmarks would convert them into magnitude-bound diagnostics. *Bridging to the frequency-lower-bound lineage*: a layered theory composing Kalai-Vempala-style frequency-floor results with the goal-coupling magnitude-ceiling here would give the field a complete two-axis picture. Other directions: moderate-to-large $I$ extension of the local FR bound; quantitative refinements of $\kappa_{\text{processing}}$ for architectures with vanishing-magnitude per-source non-degeneracy (state-collapsed SSMs, low-weight sparse attention); system-level Class 2 cases where Class 1 / Class 3 components combine.
