## Conclusion ^sec-conclusion

The opening question — *how much can an LLM be pulled away from what its evidence supports by the goal it is asked to satisfy?* — is the question the existing hallucination-theory lineage doesn't engage. The lineage bounds *frequency*; the question is about *size*. The two axes are orthogonal. A coupled-architecture model in (PI)-compatible operating regime can simultaneously have an inevitable-frequency hallucination floor (training-data structure, calibration constraints, learnability bottlenecks, computability obstructions) *and* a bounded-magnitude goal-coupling displacement on each event (geometric structure). The two together give a more complete picture than either alone.

What the geometric route delivers — and what the architecture-engaged commitment buys. The chain-rule move on the post-update law composes two literatures that hadn't met. Track 2 under (PI)+(R)+(K) gives a universal, dimension-free $\sqrt{2}$ constant by Čencov uniqueness; the no-go on Euclidean chart norms makes that commitment load-bearing rather than coincidental. The architectural classification reads the bound operationally: $\kappa_{\text{processing}} \times \mathcal{A}$ — coupling strength times residual ambiguity — with Class 1 (Separated) automatic by data-processing, Class 2 (Partial) and Class 3 (Coupled) named structural commitments. Plain decoder-only transformer attention is structurally Class 3 by [[#^lem-attention-coupled]], not as an incidental property of training.

**Limitations.** The bounds are conditional theorems. Regularity hypotheses (H2$'$) and (H4$'$) restrict the regime each Track applies in; (PI) is an axiomatic commitment, not a derivation; the architectural factorization requires (H$_\kappa$) at corollary level. The paper is theory-only — we do not instrument a deployed LLM's belief-update dynamics. The mitigation we offer is structural: Track 1's cascade has decades of empirical use in Bayesian inverse problems; Track 2 under (PI)+(R)+(K) is mathematically airtight via Čencov; the no-go is counterexample-grade. The bound captures *one channel* of hallucination — goal-coupling-induced displacement — which is real and structurally derived. Other channels (training-data structure, calibration, capacity) live in the orthogonal frequency lineage and are addressed there.

**Future directions.** Two are highest-leverage:

- *First instantiation of $\hat\kappa_{\text{processing}}$ on a deployed LLM.* The bound becomes operational once $\kappa^*$ is bounded for an architecture in a domain. Binary-uniform two-goal probing has a closed-form JSD estimator ([[#^thm-umbrella]]'s empirical-accessibility remark); activation-level mediation via the representation-engineering / steering-vector / mechanistic-interpretability lines gives a tighter handle on the architectural side. The natural empirical referent is *sycophancy* \citep{sharma-2023-sycophancy} — LLM responses matching user beliefs over truthful ones, demonstrated consistently across five state-of-the-art assistants. Sharma et al.'s *answer sycophancy metric* — change in accuracy under user-bias prompts — is essentially the binary-uniform two-goal probe of [[#^cor-architectural-factorization]] applied to question-answering tasks; instantiating the bound on their setup would convert the existing benchmarks into magnitude-bound diagnostics.
- *Bridging to the frequency-lower-bound lineage.* Kalai-Vempala-style results bound hallucination *frequency* from below; this paper bounds *size* from above. A layered theory composing the two — frequency-floor from training-data / calibration structure, magnitude-ceiling from goal-coupling geometry — would give the field a complete two-axis picture rather than two complementary half-pictures.

Other directions: extension of the locally-tight Fisher-Rao bound to moderate-to-large $I$ regime via compact-manifold or staged-composition arguments; quantitative refinements of $\kappa_{\text{processing}}$ for architectures whose per-source non-degeneracy holds structurally but with vanishing magnitude (state-collapsed SSMs, sparse-attention with low-weight goal-to-output edges); and formal handling of system-level Class 2 cases — retrieval-augmented LLMs, tool-use, scratchpads — where component-level Class 1 / Class 3 classifications combine into a system-level intermediate.
