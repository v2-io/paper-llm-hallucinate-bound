## Extended related work ^sec-related-work-extended

§2 Related Work names the two main lineages this paper composes (LLM hallucination theory; Bayesian inverse-problems posterior stability). This appendix covers the additional adjacent strands — Fisher-Rao Bayesian sensitivity, Owhadi-Scovel-Sullivan brittleness, mean-field variational inference stability, and the Markov-blanket apparatus from active inference — that sit close enough to require explicit cite-and-distinguish but do not resolve the goal-coupling-displacement gap.

### Strand 1: LLM hallucination theory ^sec-strand1

The recent statistical-inevitability and capacity-impossibility lineage targets the *frequency* of hallucination on architecture-agnostic grounds. \citet{kalai-2023-calibrated} establish that calibrated language models must hallucinate at a rate close to the fraction of singleton facts in the training data — a statistical lower bound that holds independently of the transformer architecture. \citet{kalai-2025-why} extend this to argue that hallucinations originate as binary-classification errors compounded by training-and-evaluation incentives that reward guessing under uncertainty. \citet{karbasi-2025-im} reduce hallucination *detection* to language identification in the Gold-Angluin framework, showing that detection is impossible from positive examples alone but feasible with expert-labeled negatives. \citet{wu-grama-szpankowski-2024} establish a VC-dimension-based no-free-lunch result for non-hallucinating learning. \citet{suzuki-2025-hallucinations} give a probabilistic positive result — hallucinations can be made statistically negligible — counterbalancing the computability-theoretic inevitability arguments. \citet{guo-2026-hallucination} frame hallucination as a rate-distortion theorem for membership testing under capacity constraints. \citet{liu-2025-hallucinations} formalize hallucinations as estimation failures with high-probability lower bounds on the hallucinate rate. \citet{chlon-2025-predictable} frame transformers as Bayesian-in-expectation-not-realization with $O(\log n)$ permutation dispersion. \citet{zeng-2026-halluguard} decompose hallucination risk into data-driven and reasoning-driven components.

Our distance from this strand: we bound a *displacement*, not a frequency, under named *geometric* assumptions, and we engage the *architecture's coupling structure* directly via $\kappa_{\text{processing}}$. The bound is not a substitute for the frequency bounds; it is an orthogonal axis. Among the recent papers, Zeng et al.'s decomposition is closest in shape to our $\kappa \times I$ factorization but differs on structural axis (training-time vs inference-time, vs our architectural-vs-informational).

### Strand 2: Bayesian inverse-problems posterior stability ^sec-strand2

The transport-inequality and Lipschitz-posterior machinery driving Track 1 is mature. \citet{stuart-2010-acta} establishes well-posedness of Bayesian inverse problems on function spaces, with Lipschitz continuity of the posterior in the data. \citet{sprungk-2020-local-lipschitz} extends this to local Lipschitz stability with respect to perturbations of the prior and the log-likelihood, in TV / Hellinger / Wasserstein / KL distances. \citet{cvetkovic-2025-upper} match the upper bounds with lower bounds, emphasizing that posterior sensitivity *increases* as the posterior concentrates. \citet{dolera-mainini-2023-aihp-lipschitz} obtain Lipschitz-continuity of probability kernels in the optimal-transport framework, with explicit constants in terms of Fisher-information functionals and weighted Poincaré constants — the closest-in-spirit prior result to Track 1's form. \citet{garbuno-inigo-2023-bayesian} develop integral-probability-metric perturbation analysis under log-Sobolev-style functional inequalities. \citet{latz-2020-well-posed-bip} provides well-posedness under weaker continuity. \citet{lie-sullivan-teckentrup-2017} give Hellinger-distance bounds for randomized forward models. \citet{hosseini-hsu-taghvaei-2024-conditional-ot} develop conditional optimal transport on function spaces for amortized Bayesian inference, with explicit regularity estimates on conditioning maps from prior to posterior — the closest single-paper match for Track 1's cascade. \citet{dolera-favaro-mainini-2024-ptrf-wasserstein} derive Wasserstein-based posterior contraction rates. \citet{delmoral-2026-contraction} gives Sinkhorn / Schrödinger bridge contraction via log-Sobolev — most recent transport-inequality-plus-LSI work, adjacent to Track 1's machinery.

Each of these results establishes a form of Lipschitz-in-data continuity of the posterior under increasingly weak hypotheses. [[#^thm-stuart-school-reduction]] shows that any such Lipschitz-posterior result, combined with $T_2$ concentration on the goal-induced parameter measure and slice-wise sub-Gaussianity of the conditional data law, is a sufficient verification path for Track 1's (H2$'$) on the post-update model law in any Class 1 (Separated) architecture, with cascade constant of the canonical Stuart-school form $\propto L_{\text{post}}^2/\rho_{\text{noise}}$ — tight at $2L_{\text{post}}^2/\rho_{\text{noise}}$ on conjugate-Gaussian and exponential-family-conjugate instances, with absolute multiplicative overhead from the structural kernel-tensorization step in general. Hosseini-Hsu-Taghvaei is the closest single-paper match for the amortized-inference framing; the structural-slot match holds for the entire strand.

Our distance from this strand: we apply this shared machinery to *belief-goal-coupled* inference under an architectural classification. None of these papers address goal-conditional bias under explicit architectural-coupling factorization. The relationship is shared-machinery, not theorem-containment — Strand 2 proves outputs (matching lower bounds, multi-metric extensions, sub-Lipschitz refinements, contraction rates, randomized forward models) outside Track 1's averaged-over-goal reach.

### Strand 3: Fisher-Rao geometry for Bayesian sensitivity ^sec-strand3

\citet{kurtek-bharath-2015-fisher-rao} provide a Fisher-Rao framework for Bayesian sensitivity analysis under $\varepsilon$-contamination classes — the closest existing Fisher-Rao deployment to ours. Same geometric machinery; different target (sensitivity vs universal-constant bias bound). \citet{lebanon-2004-cencov-campbell} extends Čencov-Campbell uniqueness to conditional information geometry — load-bearing precursor for the conditional-distribution setting in which our bound lives. \citet{cencov-1982-stat-decision} is the original uniqueness result for the Fisher metric under invariance under sufficient statistics; \citet{amari-nagaoka-2000-info-geom} give the modern treatment of the KL-to-Fisher second-order expansion. \citet{mcculloch-1989-local} and \citet{ruggeri-1993-infinitesimal} develop earlier Fisher-information-flavored sensitivity analyses; both are foundational but produce neither a universal constant nor a goal-coupling bound.

### Strand 4: Bayesian brittleness (handle carefully) ^sec-strand4

\citet{owhadi-scovel-sullivan-2015-ejs-finite-info,owhadi-scovel-sullivan-2015-siamrev-brittleness} establish that Bayesian inference is brittle under finite information in continuous settings: arbitrarily close models on the same data can yield arbitrarily different posterior conclusions, and any prior + model can be perturbed slightly to achieve any desired posterior. \citet{owhadi-2017-qualitative} develops the qualitative-robustness companion analysis.

A reader might worry that the brittleness results contradict our Track 1 stability bound. They do not. Owhadi-Scovel-Sullivan's no-go is for *arbitrary stability under finite information* in fixed metrics (TV, generalized moments) — they construct adversarial perturbations *outside* the well-posed regime. Our [[#^thm-umbrella]] (Track 1 instantiation) carries explicit (H1) + (H2$'$) hypotheses that exclude their brittleness regime: (H2$'$)'s post-update concentration is precisely the regularity that brittleness requires *failing*. The two no-gos constrain different things; our [[#^thm-no-go]] is for *universal-constant absent the (PI) + (R) + (K) triple*, which is a third, distinct statement. The honest reading: three different no-go results, three different scope conditions, none implying or contradicting the others.

### Strand 5: Adjacent recent work ^sec-strand5

\citet{sheng-2025-stability} establish stability of mean-field variational inference under strongly log-concave targets — adjacent to Track 1's transport-inequality machinery. \citet{cattiaux-2021-functional} develop functional inequalities (Poincaré, Cheeger, log-Sobolev) for perturbed measures with explicit constants — background machinery for [[#^h2-prime]]. \citet{ley-2015-distances} give Wasserstein-distance bounds for nested densities via the Stein method. \citet{cvetkovic-2023-choosing} address observation-operator choice for misspecified inverse problems. \citet{zahm-2018-certified} provide certified dimension reduction for nonlinear Bayesian inverse problems. \citet{kleijn-2006-misspecification} study misspecified Bayesian inference asymptotics; \citet{shalizi-2009-dynamics} studies dynamics of Bayesian updating under dependent data and misspecified models. \citet{seo-lee-sim-2026-tangent} derive $W_2$-stability bounds for tangent-linearized Gaussian inference on smooth manifolds — adjacent for the manifold-Bayesian-inference framing.

In LLM-stability-flavored work: \citet{xu-2025-policy} (*Policy Cliff*) analyzes RLHF stability for LLMs/LRMs through reward-policy maps; \citet{biau-2026-note} develop statistical proxies for retrieval-augmented generation; \citet{hyeon-2026-actionsufficient} address action-sufficient goal representations in RL. None of these target the goal-coupling bias quantity [[#^eq-bias-quantity]] under an architectural-coupling factorization.

### Strand 6: Active inference and Markov-blanket apparatus ^sec-strand6

The Markov-blanket apparatus from active inference \cite{friston-2013-life,friston-2019-particular-physics,friston-da-costa-2023-path-integrals} supplies the conditional-independence machinery our architectural-separation classification invokes. We adopt the Pearl-blanket reading \cite{bruineberg-dolega-dewhurst-baltieri-2022-bbs} — the technical conditional-independence statement — and explicitly do not adopt the Friston-blanket metaphysical reading. The Goal/Update Coupling Class partition (Class 1 Separated / Class 2 Partial / Class 3 Coupled), with the explicit Coupled-class scope-exit, is the structural extension that the Pearl-blanket form invites but does not by itself produce.

### Relation to existing decompositions ^sec-existing-decompositions

\citet{zeng-2026-halluguard}'s HalluGuard framework decomposes hallucination risk into *data-driven* and *reasoning-driven* components, linked respectively to training-time mismatches and inference-time instabilities. This is the closest-shape decomposition to our $\kappa_{\text{processing}} \times I$ factorization. The structural axes differ: HalluGuard's axis is *temporal* (training-time vs inference-time errors); ours is *architectural-vs-informational* (where the goal can flow vs how much it could resolve). The two decompositions live at different layers and do not reduce to each other. A model can have low data-driven hallucination (clean training) and still exhibit large goal-coupling displacement (Coupled-class architecture in a high-ambiguity regime), and vice versa.

\citet{chlon-2025-predictable} frame transformers as *Bayesian in expectation but not in realization*, with permutation-induced order effects scaling as $O(\log n)$. This is structurally distinct from our framing — we treat the architecture as performing a single goal-conditioned belief update on a fixed input, not as averaging over orderings — but the two are compatible: their Quantified Martingale Violation bounds the deviation from Bayesian-in-realization behavior, and our bound captures one specific channel (goal-coupling) of how non-Bayesian-in-realization a coupled architecture can be.

### Failed routes — pointer ^sec-failed-routes-pointer

Two derivation routes failed at structural levels and are documented in [[#^sec-failed-routes]]: (F1) Cramér-Rao inversion fails because Cramér-Rao bounds estimator variance from below, and inverting it for an upper bound on side-information-induced displacement requires a fixed-estimator-class assumption not available in coupled-architecture settings. (F2) Rate-distortion inversion fails because rate-distortion theory describes the minimum bits-per-symbol required to *represent* a source within distortion $D$, not the maximum *displacement induced* by injecting side-information into an update. The pattern: information-theoretic *lower-bound* machinery for estimator or encoder performance does not invert to upper bounds on side-information-induced displacement.
