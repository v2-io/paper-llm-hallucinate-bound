## Conjugate-Gaussian numerical comparison ^sec-conjugate-gauss-numerics

We compute the Track-family constants explicitly for the conjugate-Gaussian observation-prior pair and exhibit the regimes in which they are tight. The setup: latent $\theta \sim \mathcal{N}(0, \tau^2)$, observation channel $\Omega | \theta \sim \mathcal{N}(\theta, \sigma^2)$, goal-conditional likelihood-mean shift $\Omega | G \sim \mathcal{N}(\beta(G), \sigma^2)$ — for this minimal example we treat $\Omega | G$ as goal-determined directly; the world-state $\theta$ enters only via the agent's prior, not the observation. The architecture's update is goal-blind (Class 1 Separated, conjugate Bayesian update). Identifying $M_{\tau^+}$ with the posterior-mean parameter $\mu_+ := L_{\text{post}}\Omega$ (variance $\sigma_{\text{post}}^2 = L_{\text{post}}\sigma^2$ fixed), the post-update parameter law is $\mu_+ | G \sim \mathcal{N}(L_{\text{post}}\beta(G),\, L_{\text{post}}^2\sigma^2)$.

*Two distinct variances.* The *inside-posterior covariance* $\sigma_{\text{post}}^2 = L_{\text{post}}\sigma^2$ is the spread of the posterior measure on $\theta$ at a fixed observation. The *parameter-law variance* $L_{\text{post}}^2\sigma^2 = L_{\text{post}}\sigma_{\text{post}}^2$ is the spread of the random model state $\mu_+$ under data noise. They differ by exactly $L_{\text{post}}$. Track 1's $C_{T_2}$ is determined by the parameter-law variance, not the inside-posterior covariance; conflating them is the algebraic error this appendix corrects.

**Transferred information and $\kappa_{\text{processing}}$.** The goal-conditional law of $\Omega$ has small-$\mathrm{Var}_G(\beta)$ MI $I(G;\Omega | e, M_{\tau^-}) \approx \mathrm{Var}_G(\beta)/(2\sigma^2)$. The post-update parameter law inherits the same MI: the deterministic linear map $\Omega \mapsto L_{\text{post}}\Omega$ is invertible, so the data-processing inequality on the chain $G \to \Omega \to M_{\tau^+}$ saturates and $I(G; M_{\tau^+} | e, M_{\tau^-}) = I(G; \Omega | e, M_{\tau^-})$ exactly. The realized attenuation ratio is $\kappa_{\text{processing}}^{\text{realized}} = 1$. *This Class 1 conjugate-Gaussian setup does not exhibit architectural attenuation* — it sits at the worst-case Class 1 corner where DPI is tight; non-trivial $\kappa < 1$ requires a non-invertible data channel (e.g., quantization, dimension reduction) or a Class 2/3 architecture.

**Track 1 ([[#^thm-umbrella]]).** Route (a) — LSI on the post-update parameter law $\mathcal{N}(0, L_{\text{post}}^2\sigma^2)$ at small $\mathrm{Var}_G(\beta)$ — gives $\rho_{\text{post}} = 1/(L_{\text{post}}^2\sigma^2)$ and

$$C_{T_2} \;=\; 2/\rho_{\text{post}} \;=\; 2\,L_{\text{post}}^2\sigma^2 \;=\; \frac{2\,\tau^4\sigma^2}{(\sigma^2+\tau^2)^2}.$$

[[#^thm-umbrella]]: $\mathbb{E}[W_2^2] \le C_{T_2}\cdot I(G; M_{\tau^+}| e, M_{\tau^-}) = 2L_{\text{post}}^2\sigma^2 \cdot I(G;\Omega | e, M_{\tau^-})$ (using $\kappa = 1$). Equivalently, identifying $\rho_{\text{noise}} := 1/\sigma^2$ as the *noise concentration* of the conditional data law $\Omega \mid \theta$ (the quantity that enters the Stuart-school cascade's denominator — distinct from the LSI constant of the marginal observation law $P_\Omega \sim \mathcal{N}(0, \sigma^2+\tau^2)$, which would be $1/(\sigma^2+\tau^2)$),

$$\mathbb{E}[W_2^2] \;\leq\; \frac{2\,L_{\text{post}}^2}{\rho_{\text{noise}}} \cdot I(G;\Omega | e, M_{\tau^-})$$

— the canonical Stuart-school cascade constant, recovered as $C_{T_2}$ alone (DPI saturation $\kappa = 1$ is the witness that this Class 1 example sits at the Stuart corner). The general structural reduction connecting the Stuart-school hypothesis space to (H2$'$) is [[#^thm-stuart-school-reduction]]. The cascade is sharp: the slice-wise $W_2^2/\mathrm{KL}$ ratio equals $C_{T_2}$ exactly for these Gaussian parameter laws. Limit checks: prior-dominant ($\tau \ll \sigma$): $C_{T_2} \approx 2\tau^4/\sigma^2 \to 0$; likelihood-dominant ($\sigma \ll \tau$): $C_{T_2} \approx 2\sigma^2 \to 0$; balanced ($\tau = \sigma = 1$): $C_{T_2} = 1/2$ and $\mathbb{E}[W_2^2] \le I_M/2$.

**Track 2 ([[#^thm-umbrella]]).** Fisher metric on the parameter-law manifold $\{\mathcal{N}(\mu, L_{\text{post}}^2\sigma^2) : \mu \in \mathbb{R}\}$ (laws over the random parameter $\mu_+$, not posterior measures on $\theta$): $\mathbf{I}(\mu) = 1/(L_{\text{post}}^2\sigma^2)$. Fisher-Rao distance $d_{FR}(\mathcal{N}(\mu_1, v), \mathcal{N}(\mu_2, v)) = |\mu_1-\mu_2|/\sqrt v = |\mu_1-\mu_2|/(L_{\text{post}}\sigma)$. Slice-wise to the marginal $\mathcal{N}(0, L_{\text{post}}^2\sigma^2)$: $d_{FR}(P_{M_{\tau^+}| G=g}, P_{M_{\tau^+}}) \approx |\beta(g)|/\sigma$ at small $\mathrm{Var}_G(\beta)$. [[#^thm-umbrella]] (unconditional): $\mathbb{E}[d_{FR}^2] \le 2\cdot I(G; M_{\tau^+}| e, M_{\tau^-}) = \mathrm{Var}_G(\beta)/\sigma^2$ — sharp on this family.

**Track 2 Euclidean translation ([[#^thm-conjugate-gauss-euclidean]]).** $|\Delta\mu_+|_{\text{Eucl}} = L_{\text{post}}\sigma \cdot d_{FR}$, so $\mathbb{E}\,|\Delta\mu_+|_{\text{Eucl}} \le L_{\text{post}}\sigma\sqrt{2 I_M}$. The prefactor $L_{\text{post}}\sigma = \tau^2\sigma/(\sigma^2+\tau^2)$ is bounded above by $\tau/2$ uniformly (AM-GM at $\sigma=\tau$) and *vanishes in both extreme limits*: $L_{\text{post}}\sigma \to 0$ as $\sigma \to \infty$ (very noisy observations: the architecture learns essentially nothing from $\Omega$, posterior-mean displacement vanishes); $L_{\text{post}}\sigma \to 0$ as $\sigma \to 0$ (sharp observations: $L_{\text{post}}\to 1$ but $\sigma \to 0$). Maximum at balanced $\sigma=\tau$. The naive "$\sigma\sqrt{2I}$" reading is wrong (correct prefactor is $L_{\text{post}}\sigma$, not bare $\sigma$); equally importantly, the "$\sigma_{\text{post}}\sqrt{2I}$" reading is *also* wrong (it conflates inside-posterior covariance $\sigma_{\text{post}}^2 = L_{\text{post}}\sigma^2$ with parameter-law variance $L_{\text{post}}^2\sigma^2$).

**Hellinger backstop ([[#^thm-hellinger]]).** [[#^thm-hellinger]]: $\mathbb{E}\,\mathrm{Hel}(P_{M_{\tau^+}| G}, P_{M_{\tau^+}}) \le (1/\sqrt 2)\sqrt{I_M}$ globally, where $\mathrm{Hel}$ is between two parameter laws $\mathcal{N}(\mu_1, L_{\text{post}}^2\sigma^2)$ and $\mathcal{N}(\mu_2, L_{\text{post}}^2\sigma^2)$. Hellinger is globally bounded by 1, so the bound is non-trivial when $\sqrt{I_M/2} < 1$ — i.e., $I_M < 2$ nat.

Track 1 and the Track 2 Euclidean translation agree exactly at balanced scale ($\mathbb{E}\,W_2 = \mathbb{E}\,\|\Delta\mu_+\|_{\text{Eucl}} = 1/\sqrt 2$ at $I_M = 1$) — they bound the same Euclidean displacement; the Track 2 Euclidean translation is Track 2 with the Fisher-Rao-to-Euclidean conversion factor $L_{\text{post}}\sigma$ on the parameter chart. The Euclidean translation goes to zero in both extreme limits (vanishing prior-dominant and vanishing likelihood-dominant). At $I_M = 1$ nat the Hellinger bound saturates the global $\sqrt{1/2} \approx 0.71$; for $I_M \ll 1$ Track 1 / Track 2 Euclidean are tighter; for $I_M \gtrsim 2$ the Hellinger backstop is the only non-vacuous bound.
