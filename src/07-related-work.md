## Related work ^sec-related-work

### Strand 1 — LLM hallucination theory ^sec-related-work-strand1

The recent statistical-inevitability and capacity-impossibility lineage targets the *frequency* of hallucination on architecture-agnostic grounds. \citet{kalai-vempala-2023} establish that calibrated language models must hallucinate at a rate close to the fraction of singleton facts in the training data — a statistical lower bound that holds independently of the transformer architecture. \citet{kalai-nachum-vempala-zhang-2026} extend this to argue that hallucinations originate as binary-classification errors compounded by training-and-evaluation incentives that reward guessing under uncertainty. \citet{karbasi-montasser-sous-velegkas-2025} reduce hallucination *detection* to language identification in the Gold-Angluin framework, showing that detection is impossible from positive examples alone but feasible with expert-labeled negatives. \citet{wu-grama-szpankowski-2024} establish a VC-dimension-based no-free-lunch result for non-hallucinating learning. \citet{suzuki-he-tian-wang-2025} give a probabilistic positive result — hallucinations can be made statistically negligible — counterbalancing the computability-theoretic inevitability arguments. \citet{guo-li-2026} frame hallucination as a rate-distortion theorem for membership testing under capacity constraints. \citet{liu-hu-zhang-song-liu-2025} formalize hallucinations as estimation failures with high-probability lower bounds on the hallucinate rate. \citet{chlon-karim-chlon-2025} frame transformers as Bayesian-in-expectation-not-realization with $O(\log n)$ permutation dispersion. \citet{zeng-etal-2026} decompose hallucination risk into data-driven and reasoning-driven components.

Our distance from this strand: we bound a *displacement*, not a frequency, under named *geometric* assumptions, and we engage the *architecture's coupling structure* directly via $\kappa_{\text{processing}}$. The bound is not a substitute for the frequency bounds; it is an orthogonal axis. Among the recent papers, Zeng et al.'s decomposition is closest in shape to our $\kappa \times I$ factorization but differs on structural axis (training-time vs inference-time, vs our architectural-vs-informational).

### Strand 2 — Bayesian inverse-problems posterior stability (the Stuart school) ^sec-related-work-strand2

The transport-inequality and Lipschitz-posterior machinery driving Track 1 is mature. \citet{stuart-2010} establishes well-posedness of Bayesian inverse problems on function spaces, with Lipschitz continuity of the posterior in the data. \citet{sprungk-2020} extends this to local Lipschitz stability with respect to perturbations of the prior and the log-likelihood, in TV / Hellinger / Wasserstein / KL distances. \citet{cvetkovic-lie-2025} match the upper bounds with lower bounds, emphasizing that posterior sensitivity *increases* as the posterior concentrates. \citet{dolera-mainini-2023} obtain Lipschitz-continuity of probability kernels in the optimal-transport framework, with explicit constants in terms of Fisher-information functionals and weighted Poincaré constants — the closest-in-spirit prior result to Track 1's form. \citet{garbuno-inigo-etal-2023} develop integral-probability-metric perturbation analysis under log-Sobolev-style functional inequalities. \citet{latz-2020} provides well-posedness under weaker continuity. \citet{lie-sullivan-teckentrup-2017} give Hellinger-distance bounds for randomized forward models. \citet{hosseini-hsu-taghvaei-2025} develop conditional optimal transport on function spaces for amortized Bayesian inference, with explicit regularity estimates on conditioning maps from prior to posterior — the closest single-paper match for Track 1's cascade. \citet{dolera-favaro-mainini-2024} derive Wasserstein-based posterior contraction rates. \citet{del-moral-2026} gives Sinkhorn / Schrödinger bridge contraction via log-Sobolev — most recent transport-inequality-plus-LSI work, adjacent to Track 1's machinery.

Our distance from this strand: we apply this machinery to *belief-goal-coupled* inference under an architectural classification. None of these papers address goal-conditional bias under explicit architectural-coupling factorization. The closest, Hosseini-Hsu-Taghvaei, is amortized Bayesian inference (general-purpose posterior approximation) rather than goal-coupling bias; the cascade overlaps but the application differs.

### Strand 3 — Fisher-Rao geometry for Bayesian sensitivity ^sec-related-work-strand3

\citet{kurtek-bharath-2015} provide a Fisher-Rao framework for Bayesian sensitivity analysis under $\varepsilon$-contamination classes — the closest existing Fisher-Rao deployment to ours. Same geometric machinery; different target (sensitivity vs universal-constant bias bound). \citet{lebanon-2004} extends Čencov-Campbell uniqueness to conditional information geometry — load-bearing precursor for the conditional-distribution setting in which our bound lives. \citet{cencov-1982} is the original uniqueness result for the Fisher metric under invariance under sufficient statistics; \citet{amari-nagaoka-2000} give the modern treatment of the KL-to-Fisher second-order expansion. \citet{mcculloch-1989} and \citet{ruggeri-wasserman-1993} develop earlier Fisher-information-flavored sensitivity analyses; both are foundational but produce neither a universal constant nor a goal-coupling bound.

### Strand 4 — Bayesian brittleness (handle carefully) ^sec-related-work-strand4

\citet{owhadi-scovel-sullivan-2015a,owhadi-scovel-sullivan-2015b} establish that Bayesian inference is brittle under finite information in continuous settings: arbitrarily close models on the same data can yield arbitrarily different posterior conclusions, and any prior + model can be perturbed slightly to achieve any desired posterior. \citet{owhadi-scovel-2017} develop the qualitative-robustness companion analysis.

A reader might worry that the brittleness results contradict our Track 1 stability bound. They do not. Owhadi-Scovel-Sullivan's no-go is for *arbitrary stability under finite information* in fixed metrics (TV, generalized moments) — they construct adversarial perturbations *outside* the well-posed regime. Our [[#^thm-track1-uncond]] carries explicit (H1) + (H2$'$) hypotheses that exclude their brittleness regime: (H2$'$)'s post-update concentration (verifiable via LSI on the post-update law or dimension-free sub-Gaussian Lipschitz concentration; the weaker $T_1$/$W_1$ form is available from bounded support but does not give the $W_2^2$ refinement) is precisely the regularity that brittleness requires *failing*. The two no-gos constrain different things; our [[#^sec-no-go]] no-go is for *universal-constant absent the (PI) + (R) + (K) triple*, which is a third, distinct statement. The honest reading: three different no-go results, three different scope conditions, none implying or contradicting the others.

### Adjacent recent work ^sec-related-work-adjacent

\citet{sheng-etal-2025} establish stability of mean-field variational inference under strongly log-concave targets — adjacent to Track 1's transport-inequality machinery. \citet{cattiaux-guillin-2021} develop functional inequalities (Poincaré, Cheeger, log-Sobolev) for perturbed measures with explicit constants — background machinery for (H2). \citet{ley-reinert-swan-2015} give Wasserstein-distance bounds for nested densities via the Stein method. \citet{cvetkovic-etal-2023} address observation-operator choice for misspecified inverse problems. \citet{zahm-etal-2018} provide certified dimension reduction for nonlinear Bayesian inverse problems. \citet{kleijn-vdvaart-2006} study misspecified Bayesian inference asymptotics; \citet{shalizi-2009} studies dynamics of Bayesian updating under dependent data and misspecified models. \citet{seo-lee-sim-2026} derive $W_2$-stability bounds for tangent-linearized Gaussian inference on smooth manifolds — adjacent for the manifold-Bayesian-inference framing.

In LLM-stability-flavored work: \citet{xu-2025} (*Policy Cliff*) analyzes RLHF stability for LLMs/LRMs through reward-policy maps; \citet{su-kempe-ullrich-2024} address jailbreaking from a statistical perspective; \citet{biau-boyer-2026} develop statistical proxies for retrieval-augmented generation; \citet{hyeon-etal-2026} address action-sufficient goal representations in RL. None of these target the goal-coupling bias quantity [[#^eq-bias-quantity]] under an architectural-coupling factorization.

### Adjacent — active inference and Markov-blanket apparatus ^sec-related-work-active-inference

The Markov-blanket apparatus from active inference \cite{friston-2013,friston-2019,friston-dacosta-etal-2023,parr-dacosta-friston-2019} supplies the conditional-independence machinery our architectural-separation classification invokes. We adopt the Pearl-blanket reading \cite{bruineberg-etal-2022} — the technical conditional-independence statement — and explicitly do not adopt the Friston-blanket metaphysical reading. The Goal/Update Coupling Class partition (Class 1 Separated / Class 2 Partial / Class 3 Coupled), with the explicit Coupled-class scope-exit, is the structural extension that the Pearl-blanket form invites but does not by itself produce.
