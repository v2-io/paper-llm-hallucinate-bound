## Limitations and conclusion ^sec-limitations

### Limitations ^sec-limitations-list

We list these honestly because the paper's strategic posture depends on intellectual integrity rather than overclaiming.

1. **The bounds are conditional theorems, not unconditional ones.** [[#^thm-track1-uncond]] requires (H1) + (H2$'$); [[#^thm-track2-uncond]] requires (H1) + (H4$'$) + (PI) + (R) + (K); [[#^thm-track2-glob]] requires (H1) + (PI) only; [[#^thm-hellinger]] requires (H1) + (PI). Each hypothesis is a substantive commitment — (H1)'s statistical-manifold sub-case is approximate for LLMs whose belief states are not strictly parametric; (H2$'$)'s Talagrand $T_2$ inequality on the post-update law has two sufficient conditions for the full $W_2^2$ refinement (LSI on the post-update law, dimension-free sub-Gaussian Lipschitz concentration à la Gozlan), with bounded support giving only the weaker $T_1$/$W_1$ form; heavy-tailed post-updates without LSI or dimension-free concentration fall outside (H2$'$)'s $T_2$ scope; (H4$'$)'s uniform-locality regime restricts [[#^thm-track2-uncond]] to architectures where every goal-conditional slice is uniformly small ([[#^thm-track2-glob]] covers the regime where this fails, in the same metric, under (PI) only); (PI)'s sufficient-statistic-invariance (in its strong Markov-morphism form) is an axiom, not a derived requirement; (R) and (K) are implicit in the framing but are named explicitly to keep the locally-tight uniqueness theorem's hypotheses transparent.

2. **The architectural factorization is a corollary, not a theorem.** [[#^thm-track1-uncond]], [[#^thm-track2-uncond]], and [[#^thm-hellinger]] bound the displacement by the *transferred* goal-information $I(G; M_{\tau^+} \mid e_\tau, M_{\tau^-})$ unconditionally in the architectural classification. The architectural factorization $\kappa_{\text{processing}} \cdot I(G; \Omega \mid e, M)$ requires the additional architectural-bandwidth hypothesis (H$_\kappa$) at corollary level ([[#^cor-track1-arch]], [[#^cor-track2-arch]] + the architectural form of [[#^thm-hellinger]]). For Class 1 (Separated), (H$_\kappa$) holds automatically by data processing on the Markov chain $G \to \Omega \to M_{\tau^+}$. For Class 2 (Partial) and Class 3 (Coupled), (H$_\kappa$) is a named structural commitment with two documented sufficient conditions (goal-blind effective kernel; architectural-channel bandwidth bound) and a worst-case witness (parrot architecture: $\kappa_{\text{processing}} = \infty$, explicitly outside scope). The math is unconditional in architecture; the architecture-engaged reading is conditional on (H$_\kappa$).

3. **(PI) + (R) + (K) is load-bearing for the universal constant.** [[#^sec-no-go]]'s no-go shows that an intrinsic-metric commitment is necessary for the universal-constant route. [[#^thm-fr-uniqueness]] shows that (PI) at full Markov-morphism strength, plus Riemannian structure (R), plus KL as the bound's information coordinate (K), uniquely forces Fisher-Rao with constant $\sqrt{2}$ — no further freedom. (PI) is the load-bearing axiom; (R) is implicit in the Riemannian framing of [[#^sec-track1]] / [[#^sec-track2]]; (K) is built into the cascade by Step 1's KL chain rule. A reader who declines (PI) but accepts a different intrinsic-metric route (e.g., TV with Pinsker, Hellinger-as-divergence) gets a chart-independent bound but loses second-order tightness or the uniqueness within an $f$-divergence family that (R) buys. The framework's universal-$\sqrt{2}$ constant is conditional on the triple, of which (PI) is the structural axiom that the [[#^sec-track2]] derivation foregrounds.

4. **No empirical validation.** The paper is theory-only. We do not instrument an LLM's belief-update dynamics, do not estimate $\kappa_{\text{processing}}$ on a real architecture, and do not measure transferred goal-information $I(G; M_{\tau^+} \mid e, M_{\tau^-})$ in a deployed system. The empirical estimator $\hat{\kappa}_{\text{processing}}$ via two-goal probing ([[#^sec-attenuation-ratio]]) is the natural next step but is out of scope. Mitigation: the Track 1 cascade rests on textbook Talagrand $T_2$ machinery (Otto-Villani 2000 plus standard sufficient conditions); Track 2 under (PI) + (R) + (K) is mathematically airtight via Čencov uniqueness; the no-go is counterexample-grade; the Hellinger backstop's underlying inequality (\citealt[Lemma 2.4]{tsybakov-2009}) is global without regularity.

5. **The Goal/Update Coupling Class is a coarse partition.** The three-class taxonomy (Separated / Partial / Coupled) captures the architectural distinction at a structural level but elides finer-grained variations (depth-of-coupling, layer-wise variation, heterogeneous attention patterns). A more refined taxonomy could presumably parameterize $\kappa_{\text{processing}}$ in terms of architectural depth and connectivity. We treat this as future work; the present three-class partition with [[#^thm-attention-coupled]]'s structural Coupled-class derivation is what is needed to ground the bound.

6. **The bound captures one channel of hallucination.** Goal-coupling-induced displacement is a real channel — supported by [[#^thm-attention-coupled]]'s structural Coupled-class derivation showing how transformer attention routes the goal through every layer — but it is one channel among several. Training-data structure, calibration constraints, learnability bottlenecks, and computability obstructions \cite{kalai-vempala-2023,kalai-nachum-vempala-zhang-2026,karbasi-montasser-sous-velegkas-2025,wu-grama-szpankowski-2024,suzuki-he-tian-wang-2025} address other channels. The bound is part of the picture, not the whole picture.

7. **Bias-bound interpretation depends on in-context-learning correspondence.** [[#^thm-attention-coupled]]'s structural Coupled-class claim is robust — it is a property of attention connectivity, not of a token-distribution-as-belief-state idealization. The bias-bound *application* to a posterior interpretation invokes the in-context-learning correspondence \cite{garg-etal-2022,akyurek-etal-2023,vonoswald-etal-2023,xie-etal-2022}, which has its own scope (in-distribution prompts: the correspondence is tight; OOD/jailbreak: the correspondence degrades to a bound on output-distribution displacement rather than belief-state displacement). The structural claim and the application claim are separable.

### Conclusion ^sec-conclusion

We have shown that the goal-conditional bias in belief-goal-coupled inference admits two layers of upper bound: an *unconditional* umbrella theorem in transferred goal-information [[#^eq-umbrella]] with $C$ supplied by either the transport-inequality cascade ([[#^thm-track1-uncond]], $C = \sqrt{C_{T_2}}$ on $W_2$ under (H1) + (H2$'$); $C_{T_2}$ recovers $2 L_{\text{post}}^2/\rho_{\text{LSI}}$ in the conjugate-Gaussian case via LSI on the post-update) or the Fisher-Rao route — locally tight ([[#^thm-track2-uncond]], $C = \sqrt{2}$ on $d_{FR}$ under (H1) + (H4$'$) + (PI) + (R) + (K)), globally valid ([[#^thm-track2-glob]], $C = \pi/\sqrt{2}$ on $d_{FR}$ under (H1) + (PI) only), or directly on the chord ([[#^thm-hellinger]], $C = 1/\sqrt{2}$ on Hellinger under (H1) + (PI), no small-information restriction); and an *architectural corollary* under the coupling-attenuation hypothesis (H$_\kappa$) [[#^eq-arch-corollary]], recovering the κ × 𝒜 factorization that gives the bound its operational reading. (H$_\kappa$) is automatic for Class 1 (Separated) by data processing and a named structural commitment for Class 2 (Partial) and Class 3 (Coupled) with documented sufficient conditions and a worst-case witness.

A scale-family counterexample ([[#^thm-no-go]]) shows that no coordinate-independent universal constant exists for Euclidean chart norms — making the (PI) + (R) + (K) commitment load-bearing for [[#^thm-track2-uncond]]'s universal $\sqrt{2}$ rather than coincidental. [[#^thm-fr-uniqueness]] (uniqueness) shows the triple uniquely forces Fisher-Rao with that constant; alternative chart-independent routes (TV-Pinsker, Hellinger-as-divergence) give weaker bounds. [[#^thm-conjugate-gauss-euclidean]] (conjugate-Gaussian Euclidean) defuses a natural reviewer reaction: within parametric families, the Euclidean translation carries prefactor $L_{\text{post}}\sigma = \tau^2\sigma/(\sigma^2+\tau^2)$, bounded by $\tau/2$ uniformly and *vanishing* in both extreme observation-noise limits — a strictly stronger defusing of the no-go's scale-family pathology than a posterior-precision reading would suggest. The Goal/Update Coupling Class partition — Class 1 (Separated), Class 2 (Partial), Class 3 (Coupled), monotonic in coupling strength — carries the $\kappa_{\text{processing}}$ coefficient under (H$_\kappa$); [[#^thm-attention-coupled]] derives the structural Coupled-class status of plain decoder-only transformer attention from attention connectivity, independently of the token-distribution-as-belief-state idealization that the bias-bound *interpretation* additionally invokes.

The paper composes two literatures that have not been composed: the LLM-hallucination-theory literature, which has the right target but has not engaged the architecture's coupling structure geometrically, and the Bayesian-inverse-problems literature, which has the geometric machinery but has not been applied to belief-goal-coupled inference. The composition is the contribution; the architectural classification, the four theorems ([[#^thm-track1-uncond]] transport-inequality, [[#^thm-track2-uncond]] locally-tight Fisher-Rao under (PI) + (R) + (K) + (H4$'$), [[#^thm-track2-glob]] globally-valid Fisher-Rao under (PI) only, [[#^thm-hellinger]] chord-companion Hellinger), the uniqueness theorem ([[#^thm-fr-uniqueness]]), the no-go ([[#^thm-no-go]]), [[#^lem-chord-arc]] (global chord-arc bound), and the structural Coupled-class lemma ([[#^thm-attention-coupled]]) are what make the composition do work.

Future directions include: empirical estimation of $\kappa_{\text{processing}}$ for deployed LLMs via two-goal probing; extension of [[#^thm-track2-uncond]] to the large-information regime via compact-manifold or staged-composition arguments; instantiation of the bound for specific architecture families (transformer, mixture-of-experts, retrieval-augmented); and connection to the frequency-lower-bound lineage of Kalai-Vempala-style results to give a layered hallucination theory in which structural-bias bounds at the per-event level compose with frequency bounds at the corpus level.
