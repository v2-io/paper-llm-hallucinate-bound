## Discussion ^sec-discussion

### What the bound says, and what it doesn't ^sec-bias-baseline

The bound [[#^eq-umbrella]] is a *bias-bound theorem*: it bounds the size of the goal-conditional displacement of the model state, conditional on named geometric assumptions. It is not a frequency lower bound in the lineage of \cite{kalai-vempala-2023} or \cite{kalai-nachum-vempala-zhang-2026}, a learnability-theoretic impossibility in the lineage of \cite{wu-grama-szpankowski-2024} or \cite{karbasi-montasser-sous-velegkas-2025}, or a computability-theoretic inevitability in the lineage of \cite{suzuki-he-tian-wang-2025}. The four results address different axes of the hallucination problem.

We see them as complementary, not competing. Kalai-Vempala bound the *fraction* of training-singleton facts on which a calibrated model must hallucinate; we bound the *size* of the displacement induced by goal-coupling on a given event. Kalai-Nachum-Vempala-Zhang attribute hallucination to training-and-evaluation incentives (binary-classification error plus reward for guessing); we attribute *one specific structural channel* — goal-conditional reweighting through architectural coupling — to a quantitative bound under named geometric assumptions. Both can be true. A coupled-architecture model in (PI)-compatible operating regime can simultaneously have an inevitable-frequency hallucination floor (training-data structure) *and* a bounded-magnitude goal-coupling displacement on each event (geometric structure). The two together give a more complete picture than either alone.

**What baseline does the bound measure displacement from?** The formal baseline of [[#^eq-umbrella]] is the *goal-marginal* post-update law $P_{M_{\tau^+}\mid e_\tau, M_{\tau^-}}$ — the operating-distribution average over goals. Two operationally distinct readings sit on top of this:

*(i) Goal-pairwise dispersion (direct from [[#^thm-track1-uncond]], no extra assumption).* By the triangle inequality on $W_2$, two random goals $g, g' \sim P_G$ produce post-update laws at most $2\sqrt{C_{T_2}\cdot I(G;M_{\tau^+}\mid e, M_{\tau^-})}$ apart on average:

$$\mathbb{E}_{g, g' \sim P_G}\bigl[W_2(P_{M_{\tau^+}\mid G=g, e, M_{\tau^-}},\, P_{M_{\tau^+}\mid G=g', e, M_{\tau^-}})\bigr] \;\le\; 2\sqrt{C_{T_2}\cdot I(G;M_{\tau^+}\mid e, M_{\tau^-})}.$$ ^eq-pairwise-dispersion

This is *direct* from [[#^thm-track1-uncond]] + triangle, no operating-distribution assumption needed. Operational reading: how much does swapping goals shift the architecture's response? A tight bound on this is the cleanest "goal-coupling pulls the response by at most this much, measured between goal-conditional outcomes" reading.

*(ii) Evidence-only reading (under a named architectural commitment (H$_{\text{neutral}}$)).* For an *evidence-supported posterior* baseline — the law one would obtain from a hypothetical goal-blind variant of the architecture — an additional architectural commitment is needed:

> [!hypothesis] (H$_{\text{neutral}}$) Identifiable neutral goal ^def-h-neutral
> There exists a "neutral goal" $G_0$ in the goal space such that the goal-conditional law $P_{M_{\tau^+}\mid G=G_0, e, M_{\tau^-}}$ coincides with (or approximates within tolerance $\epsilon$) the post-update law of a goal-blind variant of the architecture, denoted $P_{M_{\tau^+}\mid e, M_{\tau^-}}^{\text{ev-only}}$.

For Class 1 (Separated), (H$_{\text{neutral}}$) is *automatic* with $\epsilon = 0$: the architecture's update is goal-blind by construction, so $P_{M_{\tau^+}\mid G,e,M_{\tau^-}}$ doesn't depend on $G$ and any choice of $G_0$ trivially gives $P_{M_{\tau^+}\mid G_0,e,M_{\tau^-}} = P_{M_{\tau^+}\mid e,M_{\tau^-}}^{\text{ev-only}}$. For Class 2/3, (H$_{\text{neutral}}$) is a substantive operational commitment: the architecture has a "default" or "neutral" goal mode (a system-prompt placeholder; an "off" position on a goal channel; a goal-input set to a designated reference) under which it behaves as a goal-blind variant. This is plausible for many deployed LLMs (default temperatures, neutral system prompts, unbiased instruction priors) but it is not a derived property — it is an architectural commitment about what counts as the architecture's "evidence-only" behavior.

Under (H$_{\text{neutral}}$) at tolerance $\epsilon$, the evidence-only displacement is bounded:

$$\mathbb{E}_{g \sim P_G}\bigl[W_2(P_{M_{\tau^+}\mid G=g, e, M_{\tau^-}},\, P_{M_{\tau^+}\mid e,M_{\tau^-}}^{\text{ev-only}})\bigr] \;\le\; \sqrt{C_{T_2}\cdot I(G;M_{\tau^+}\mid e, M_{\tau^-})} \,+\, \sqrt{C_{T_2}\cdot \mathrm{KL}_{G_0}} \,+\, \epsilon,$$ ^eq-evidence-only

where $\mathrm{KL}_{G_0} := \mathrm{KL}(P_{M_{\tau^+}\mid G=G_0, e, M_{\tau^-}}\,\Vert\, P_{M_{\tau^+}\mid e, M_{\tau^-}})$ is the slice-wise KL at the neutral goal — bounded by $\delta_\star$ under (H4$'$-unif), and $\le 2I$ under the symmetry $\mathrm{KL}_{G_0} \le \mathrm{ess\,sup}_g\mathrm{KL}_g$. In the (H4$'$-unif) regime, this gives the clean evidence-only bound $\mathbb{E}\,W_2 \le 2\sqrt{C_{T_2}\delta_\star} + \epsilon$. For Class 1 (where $\epsilon = 0$ and $\mathrm{KL}_{G_0} = 0$), this collapses to [[#^thm-track1-uncond]]'s bound itself: the *operational* operating-distribution baseline coincides with the *causal* evidence-only baseline.

The two readings are operationally distinct. The pairwise-dispersion reading (i) is what an operator can directly measure (run the same architecture on the same evidence under different goals and compare). The evidence-only reading (ii) requires (H$_{\text{neutral}}$) — the operator must additionally commit to what counts as the architecture's "neutral" goal-mode for the evidence-only baseline to be defined. This is mathematically honest about what the bound delivers: a direct goal-pairwise-dispersion result without operational assumptions; an evidence-only-displacement result under a named (H$_{\text{neutral}}$). When prose elsewhere in this paper refers to displacement from "the answer the evidence supports," the formal claim is the (H$_{\text{neutral}}$)-conditional reading (ii); the framework's machinery delivers (i) unconditionally.

### Class 3 (Coupled) architectures and LLMs ^sec-attention-coupled

A plain decoder-only transformer performing in-context inference is *Class 3 (Coupled) by construction* in a precise structural sense:

> [!lemma] Coupled-class attention connectivity ^thm-attention-coupled
> Let $\mathcal{G}_\theta$ be the directed computational graph of an autoregressive transformer with parameters $\theta$, taking input sequence $X_{1:t-1}$ and producing internal activations $\{h_\ell^{(i)}\}_{\ell, i}$ across layers $\ell$ and positions $i$. If positions $i_G \subseteq \{1, \ldots, t-1\}$ contain goal/prompt tokens and positions $i_E$ contain evidence tokens with $i_G \cap i_E = \emptyset$, then for every layer $\ell \geq 1$ and position $j \geq \max(i_G \cup i_E)$, the activation $h_\ell^{(j)}$ has a directed-graph path from at least one $i_G$ position whenever attention weights are non-degenerate on the $i_G$ row.

> [!proof]
> By induction on layer depth. Layer $0$ is the embedding: $h_0^{(i)}$ depends only on $X_i$. At layer $\ell \geq 1$, a standard transformer block (pre-LN or post-LN) computes the residual update [[#^eq-transformer-block]], where LayerNorm and the MLP are *position-wise* (the operation at position $i$ depends only on $h_{\ell-1}^{(i)}$, not on other positions). This induces a directed position-graph with two edge types at each layer: (i) a *same-position* residual edge $h_{\ell-1}^{(j)} \to h_\ell^{(j)}$ always present (via the residual stream); (ii) *cross-position* attention edges $h_{\ell-1}^{(i)} \to h_\ell^{(j)}$ present whenever $\mathrm{Attn}_\ell^{(j,i)}(\theta) \ne 0$. Position-wise LayerNorm and MLP preserve both edge types. Inductively, every input position $i \le j$ has a directed-graph path to $h_\ell^{(j)}$ for every $\ell \ge 0$: the same-position residual chain gives $i = j$; non-degenerate attention at any single layer along the path gives $i \ne j$. In particular, for any goal position $i_G \le j$, $h_\ell^{(j)}$ has a path back to position $i_G$ in the input whenever attention is non-degenerate on the $(j, i_G)$ edge at some layer $\ell' \le \ell$. RMSNorm, GroupNorm, and FlashAttention preserve this position-graph (position-wise normalization in the first two cases; mathematically-equivalent attention implementation in the third). Causal masking preserves $\mathrm{Attn}_\ell^{(j, i_G)} \ne 0$ for $i_G \le j$ — the regime the lemma covers. Sliding-window or sparse attention preserves connectivity by composition across layers when the window/sparsity pattern admits a multi-hop path from $i_G$ to $j$, which is the case for trained LLMs in their operating regime. Architectures with explicit goal-to-output attention masking, or token-dropping at intermediate layers, fall outside the lemma's scope and are appropriately classified as Class 2 (Partial) per [[#^sec-architectural-classification]]. $\square$

$$h_\ell^{(j)} = h_{\ell-1}^{(j)} \;+\; \mathrm{Attn}_\ell(\mathrm{LN}(h_{\ell-1}))_{(j)} \;+\; \mathrm{MLP}_\ell(\mathrm{LN}(h_{\ell-1}^{(j)})).$$ ^eq-transformer-block

[[#^thm-attention-coupled]] establishes a *downstream-output graph-reachability* claim: at every layer $\ell \ge 1$ and every position $j \ge \max(i_G \cup i_E)$ (positions causally downstream of the goal and evidence under standard causal masking), the activation $h_\ell^{(j)}$ has a directed-graph path back to at least one goal position $i_G$, whenever attention weights are non-degenerate on the $(j, i_G)$ edge. Three caveats:

- *Graph reachability is not the same as causal-influence magnitude.* A directed-graph path with near-zero attention weights produces a formal edge with negligible quantitative influence on $h_\ell^{(j)}$. The [[#^thm-attention-coupled]] connectivity claim is therefore *structural* — about the existence of a path, not the magnitude of effect — and translates into Class 3 (Coupled) classification under the Markov-blanket conditional-independence reading of [[#^sec-architectural-classification]] but does not by itself bound $\kappa_{\text{processing}}$. Bounding $\kappa$ requires the architectural-bandwidth conditions of [[#^sec-h-kappa-status]] (information-bottleneck on the goal channel; bounded direct-channel capacity uniformly over operating distributions).

- *Causal masking restricts the lemma's scope to downstream positions.* For $j < \min(i_G)$ — positions strictly upstream of any goal token — there is no causal path from goal to $h_\ell^{(j)}$ under causal attention masks; those positions are Class 1 (Separated) in their own right. The lemma covers the standard regime where the post-update model state ($M_{\tau^+}$) is read off from a position downstream of all goal and evidence tokens (the typical autoregressive next-token-distribution interpretation).

- *Sparse and sliding-window attention require a multi-hop reachability assumption.* The lemma's connectivity proof composes single-layer attention edges across layers; for sliding-window or sparse-attention architectures, multi-hop connectivity from $i_G$ to $j$ requires at least one composition of single-layer paths through the window/sparsity pattern. We do not derive this multi-hop reachability — it is a per-architecture combinatorial fact about which positions can communicate, and is straightforward to verify case-by-case (full attention, local + global heads, trained LLMs in practice). Architectures whose sparsity pattern excludes goal-to-output communication entirely fall outside the lemma's hypothesis and are appropriately Class 2 (Partial).

This holds across three natural readings of $M_{\tau^+}$ — output distribution, KV-cache, appended context — all of which are Class 3 (Coupled) by [[#^thm-attention-coupled]] in the typical autoregressive post-goal regime.

The architectural-separation condition of [[#^sec-architectural-classification]] thus fails by construction (architectural connectivity, in the typical regime) at the graph-reachability level, even before quantitative effect-magnitude is considered. LLM *systems* with retrieval, tools, scratchpads, external memory, or verifiers add modular pathways at the system level — those are appropriately classified as Class 2 (Partial) (or system-dependent), with $\kappa_{\text{processing}}$ varying across components.

The bias bound's *interpretation* — bounding the displacement of an implicit posterior under goal-conditional re-prompting — additionally invokes a token-distribution-as-belief-state correspondence. The recent in-context-learning literature \cite{garg-etal-2022,akyurek-etal-2023,vonoswald-etal-2023,xie-etal-2022} shows transformers can implement Bayesian inference / gradient descent in-context, with the next-token distribution playing the role of a posterior output of that implicit update. Under this correspondence, the bound applies to belief-state displacement in the trained-on-in-distribution regime; it degrades to a bound on output-distribution displacement (rather than belief-state displacement) outside that regime. The structural Coupled-class claim of [[#^thm-attention-coupled]] is robust; the bias-bound *application* is conditional on the in-context-learning correspondence, which has its own scope.

This places plain transformer inference in the regime where the unconditional theorems [[#^thm-track1-uncond]] / [[#^thm-track2-uncond]] bound something real: the transferred goal-information $I(G; M_{\tau^+} \mid e, M_{\tau^-})$ is non-zero, and the relevant question is the *size* of the displacement. (H1) for Track 1 / Track 2 requires a statistical-manifold reading of the LLM's belief state; this is approximate at best — token-distribution-as-belief-state is a useful idealization. (H2$'$) for Track 1 holds in $T_1$/$W_1$ form on the simplex of token distributions via the diameter bound ($D = \sqrt 2$ for the probability simplex under the standard Euclidean embedding); the *full* $T_2$/$W_2^2$ refinement of [[#^thm-track1-uncond]] is *not* available from bare bounded support and requires either an LSI on the simplex or dimension-free sub-Gaussian concentration. The simplex's positive sectional curvature on the round-sphere image $x \mapsto \sqrt x$ supports a Bakry-Émery CD$(K, n-1)$ Fisher-Rao LSI with $K > 0$, which is more naturally treated under Track 2 — that is the route that gives the universal $\sqrt 2 \sqrt I$ form on this geometry without diameter weakening. (H4$'$) for Track 2 requires the small-transferred-information regime, which is the operating-regime model of "moderate" goal-coupling; macroscopic hallucination is covered by [[#^thm-hellinger]]'s Hellinger backstop. The architectural corollary's (H$_\kappa$) is the additional commitment that lets $\kappa_{\text{processing}}$ enter multiplicatively ([[#^sec-h-kappa-status]]); for Coupled-class architectures it is named structurally, not derived.

The empirical estimator $\hat{\kappa}_{\text{processing}}$ via two-goal probing of [[#^sec-attenuation-ratio]] is the natural diagnostic: pin a representative event set, vary the goal, measure how the model's epistemic content shifts. Under (PI), the shift's Fisher-Rao magnitude is bounded by $\sqrt{2 \cdot \hat{I}_M}$ at the operating point, where $\hat{I}_M$ is an empirical estimate of the transferred goal-information. We do not pursue this empirically here; the present paper is theory.

### Why four theorems are needed ^sec-discussion-four-theorems

A natural reaction is "[[#^thm-track1-uncond]] already gives a constant — why [[#^thm-track2-uncond]], [[#^thm-track2-glob]], and [[#^thm-hellinger]]?". Four reasons, one per theorem.

First, [[#^thm-track1-uncond]]'s constant $C_{T_2}$ is *not dimension-free*. It depends on the post-update concentration (LSI constant, dimension-free sub-Gaussian variance proxy à la Gozlan), which varies across architectures and tasks. The Fisher-Rao constants of [[#^thm-track2-uncond]] and [[#^thm-track2-glob]] are dimension-free under (PI).

Second, [[#^thm-track1-uncond]] requires (H2$'$) — a Talagrand $T_2$ inequality on the post-update law. Heavy-tailed post-updates without LSI and without dimension-free sub-Gaussian Lipschitz concentration fail (H2$'$); the $T_1$/$W_1$ fallback under bounded support remains available but at weaker constants. [[#^thm-track2-uncond]] under the (PI) + (R) + (K) triple does not require $T_2$; it requires (H4$'$) uniform-locality and a statistical-manifold sub-case. These are different scope conditions, and there are architectures (heavy-tailed post-updates without $T_2$, but uniformly moderate goal-coupling on a statistical-manifold) where [[#^thm-track2-uncond]] applies and [[#^thm-track1-uncond]]'s $W_2^2$ form does not.

Third, [[#^thm-track2-uncond]] requires the uniform-locality hypothesis (H4$'$) — every conditional slice $\mathrm{KL}_g$ uniformly small, not just the average. When (H4$'$) fails (rare goals carry disproportionately large transferred information, or the operating distribution genuinely sits at moderate-to-large $I$), [[#^thm-track2-uncond]]'s slice-wise expansion is no longer uniformly sharp. [[#^thm-track2-glob]] covers this regime in the *same* metric (Fisher-Rao), at the cost of $\sqrt 2 \mapsto \pi/\sqrt 2$ on the constant — strictly nested hypotheses (drop (H4$'$) + (R) + (K), keep (PI)). Worth its own statement because the (PI)-only result is structurally distinct: it works on architectures where (R) and (K) are difficult to certify directly.

Fourth, [[#^thm-hellinger]] provides a *direct* Hellinger statement — the chord companion to [[#^thm-track2-glob]]'s arc. Useful because Hellinger admits the cleanest universal constant ($1/\sqrt 2$ vs. Fisher-Rao's $\pi/\sqrt 2$), is bounded globally by 1 (so the bound's non-vacuity threshold is structural), and is the natural target metric for downstream estimators that work directly with the chord rather than the geodesic arc.

These four theorems together cover the parametric, dimension-free, macroscopic, and chord regimes; each carries its own scope conditions, named explicitly. [[#^sec-no-go]]'s no-go shows that *some* coordinate-invariance commitment is necessary for the dimension-free constants — without (PI), none of [[#^thm-track2-uncond]], [[#^thm-track2-glob]], or [[#^thm-hellinger]] is available; with (PI), all three are, with strictly nested additional hypotheses.

### Status of the coupling-attenuation hypothesis (H$_\kappa$) ^sec-h-kappa-status

The unconditional theorems [[#^thm-track1-uncond]] and [[#^thm-track2-uncond]] bound the displacement directly by *transferred* goal-information $I(G; M_{\tau^+} \mid e_\tau, M_{\tau^-})$ — the actually-realized coupling between $G$ and the post-update model state — without any architectural commitment. (H$_\kappa$) enters only at the architectural-corollary level ([[#^cor-track1-arch]], [[#^cor-track2-arch]]): under [[#^sec-attenuation-ratio]]'s definition $\kappa_{\text{processing}} := I(G; M_{\tau^+}\mid e,M)/I(G; \Omega_\tau\mid e,M)$, the substitution

$$I(G; M_{\tau^+} \mid e_\tau, M_{\tau^-}) \;=\; \kappa_{\text{processing}} \cdot I(G; \Omega_\tau \mid e_\tau, M_{\tau^-})$$ ^eq-kappa-substitution

is *definitional*. The corollary's bound $\mathbb{E}\,\Vert\Delta M_\text{bias}\Vert \le C\sqrt{\kappa_{\text{processing}} \cdot I(G;\Omega\mid e,M)}$ then follows immediately from the unconditional theorem. What *is* the architectural commitment named (H$_\kappa$) is the *boundedness* of this ratio — a real structural assumption for some architecture classes:

> [!hypothesis] (H$_\kappa$) Bounded architectural attenuation ^hyp-h-kappa-restated
> $\kappa_{\text{processing}} \leq \kappa^* < \infty$, where $\kappa^*$ is a named architectural constant (typically $1$ for non-amplifying architectures).

The architectural-corollary form is the contribution-headline; the unconditional theorem is the structural backbone. A reader who declines (H$_\kappa$) keeps the unconditional bound — they lose the κ × 𝒜 factorization but not the math (and the bound just becomes "use the realized $\kappa_{\text{processing}}$").

**Class 1 (Separated) — automatic by data processing.** Under architectural separation, the post-update map factors as $M_{\tau^+} = K(M_{\tau^-}, e_\tau, \Omega_\tau)$ with no $G$-dependence beyond what flows through $\Omega_\tau$. Conditional on $(e_\tau, M_{\tau^-})$, $G \to \Omega_\tau \to M_{\tau^+}$ is a Markov chain, and the data-processing inequality gives $I(G; M_{\tau^+} \mid e, M_{\tau^-}) \leq I(G; \Omega_\tau \mid e, M_{\tau^-})$ — i.e., $\kappa_{\text{processing}} \leq 1$ automatically. (H$_\kappa$) is *not* a commitment for Class 1 (Separated); it is an automatic consequence of the architecture's architectural separation.

**Class 2 (Partial) and Class 3 (Coupled) — named structural commitment.** Under Class 3 (Coupled), the post-update map depends on $G$ directly via the architectural channel (e.g., attention reading $G$ as part of its query). The Markov-chain $G \to \Omega \to M_{\tau^+}$ does *not* hold conditional on $(e, M_{\tau^-})$, because the $G$-information enters $M_{\tau^+}$ on a path that bypasses $\Omega$. Class 2 (Partial) inherits this issue on its merged pathways. Data processing on the architectural pathway does not bound $\kappa_{\text{processing}}$ automatically — the architectural channel can in principle amplify. Two clean structural conditions each give $\kappa_{\text{processing}} \leq 1$:

1. *Goal-blind effective kernel.* Suppose the post-update map factors as $M_{\tau^+} = K(M_{\tau^-}, e_\tau, \widetilde{\Omega}_\tau)$ with $K$ deterministic, where $\widetilde{\Omega}_\tau$ is a goal-conditional reweighting of $\Omega_\tau$ that is *non-amplifying*: $I(G; \widetilde{\Omega}_\tau \mid e_\tau, M_{\tau^-}) \le I(G; \Omega_\tau \mid e_\tau, M_{\tau^-})$. Equivalently, $G$'s only path to $M_{\tau^+}$ runs through $\widetilde{\Omega}_\tau$, and the reweighting does not concentrate goal-information beyond what $\Omega_\tau$ already carries. Then conditional on $(e_\tau, M_{\tau^-})$, $G \to \widetilde{\Omega}_\tau \to M_{\tau^+}$ is a Markov chain (since $K$ depends on $G$ only through $\widetilde{\Omega}_\tau$), and the data-processing inequality applied along this chain gives [[#^eq-goal-blind-kernel]], i.e., $\kappa_{\text{processing}} \le 1$.

$$I(G; M_{\tau^+}\mid e_\tau, M_{\tau^-}) \;\le\; I(G; \widetilde{\Omega}_\tau \mid e_\tau, M_{\tau^-}) \;\le\; I(G; \Omega_\tau \mid e_\tau, M_{\tau^-}).$$ ^eq-goal-blind-kernel

2. *Bounded direct architectural channel.* Suppose the architecture's *direct* goal-coupling pathway (independent of $\Omega_\tau$) has bounded conditional MI relative to the resolvable channel: $I(G; M_{\tau^+} \mid e_\tau, M_{\tau^-}, \Omega_\tau) \le \alpha \cdot I(G; \Omega_\tau \mid e_\tau, M_{\tau^-})$ for some $\alpha \ge 0$. The chain-rule of mutual information conditioned on $(e_\tau, M_{\tau^-})$ gives [[#^eq-mi-chain-rule]]; combined with $I(G; M_{\tau^+}\mid e_\tau, M_{\tau^-}) \le I(G; M_{\tau^+}, \Omega_\tau \mid e_\tau, M_{\tau^-})$ (revealing $\Omega_\tau$ as additional side information cannot decrease MI), we obtain [[#^eq-direct-channel-bound]], i.e., $\kappa_{\text{processing}} \le 1+\alpha$. For $\alpha = 0$ (architectural channel adds nothing beyond what $\Omega_\tau$ provides given $(e_\tau, M_{\tau^-})$), $\kappa_{\text{processing}} \le 1$.

$$I(G; M_{\tau^+}, \Omega_\tau \mid e_\tau, M_{\tau^-}) \;=\; I(G; \Omega_\tau \mid e_\tau, M_{\tau^-}) + I(G; M_{\tau^+} \mid e_\tau, M_{\tau^-}, \Omega_\tau).$$ ^eq-mi-chain-rule

$$I(G; M_{\tau^+}\mid e_\tau, M_{\tau^-}) \;\le\; I(G; \Omega_\tau \mid e_\tau, M_{\tau^-}) + I(G; M_{\tau^+} \mid e_\tau, M_{\tau^-}, \Omega_\tau) \;\le\; (1+\alpha)\,I(G; \Omega_\tau \mid e_\tau, M_{\tau^-}).$$ ^eq-direct-channel-bound

Neither condition holds uniformly across Class 2/3 architectures without an explicit assumption. Both conditions name *architectural* properties: condition 1 is a factorization claim about how the post-update map decomposes (architectural-only — independent of $P_G$); condition 2 names a conditional MI rate, which depends on $P_G$ unless lifted to a *uniform-over-operating-distribution* form

$$\sup_{P_G} \;\sup_{P(\Omega\mid G,e, M_{\tau^-})} \;\frac{I(G; M_{\tau^+} \mid e_\tau, M_{\tau^-}, \Omega_\tau)}{I(G; \Omega_\tau \mid e_\tau, M_{\tau^-})} \;\le\; \alpha,$$

which *is* architectural-only (a worst-case bound on the architecture's direct-channel capacity, independent of any specific operating distribution). The uniform form is what an architectural-design certificate would deliver: bounded attention fan-in, bounded mutual-information capacity at the goal-channel layer, or an explicit information-bottleneck constraint at the goal-input port. Whether the realized $\kappa_{\text{processing}}$ in deployment satisfies (H$_\kappa$) is, at the architecture level, the question of whether the architecture's worst-case channel capacity is bounded; at the operating level, the question of whether the realized goal distribution does or does not exercise the architecture into the unbounded regime. (H$_\kappa$) at the architectural level is the strong commitment; (H$_\kappa$) at the operating level is the weaker realized-ratio version. Both are valid; they differ in the universality of the implied bound.

**Worst-case witness — parrot architecture.** Consider a degenerate Coupled-class architecture whose update rule is $M_{\tau^+} := G$ (the post-update model state is identically the goal). Two computations:

*Numerator.* Since $M_{\tau^+} = G$ deterministically conditional on $(e_\tau, M_{\tau^-})$, $I(G; M_{\tau^+} \mid e_\tau, M_{\tau^-}) = H(G \mid e_\tau, M_{\tau^-})$ exactly.

*Denominator.* Suppose $G \perp\!\!\!\perp \Omega_\tau$ given $(e_\tau, M_{\tau^-})$ — the goal carries no information about the latent world-state beyond what the evidence and prior have already pinned down. (Operationally this is the "make me feel good" goal alluded to in [[#^sec-attenuation-ratio]]: the user's affective goal is independent of the empirical question the evidence resolves.) Then $I(G; \Omega_\tau \mid e_\tau, M_{\tau^-}) = 0$.

Hence $\kappa_{\text{processing}} = H(G \mid e_\tau, M_{\tau^-}) / 0 = +\infty$. (H$_\kappa$) explicitly fails. This shows (H$_\kappa$) is a *real* structural commitment for non-Separated architectures, not a free move — a degenerate Coupled-class architecture with $M_{\tau^+} = G$ on a goal independent of the latent world-state has unbounded $\kappa$. The unconditional theorems still bound the parrot architecture's displacement by transferred information directly: the prefactor in the bound's RHS is $\sqrt{H(G \mid e_\tau, M_{\tau^-})}$ rather than $\sqrt{\kappa \cdot I(G; \Omega)}$ — finite when $G$ has finite entropy. The κ × 𝒜 factorization (and hence the architectural-corollary form) does not apply.

**The honest position.** Track 1's [[#^thm-track1-uncond]] and Track 2's [[#^thm-track2-uncond]] are unconditional in the architectural classification — their math holds without (H$_\kappa$). The architectural corollaries ([[#^cor-track1-arch]], [[#^cor-track2-arch]]) recover the κ × 𝒜 factorization via the definitional substitution above; (H$_\kappa$)'s additional content is the *boundedness* of the resulting $\kappa_{\text{processing}}$ — automatic for Class 1 (Separated) by DPI, a named structural commitment for Class 2 (Partial) and Class 3 (Coupled) with documented sufficient conditions and worst-case witness. The framework's contribution is the factorization; the math survives without it.

### Failed routes recorded ^sec-discussion-failed-routes

Two derivation routes failed at structural levels and are recorded here in summary so future work does not re-attempt them. Full statements are in [[#^sec-failed-routes]].

**(F1) Cramér-Rao inversion fails.** Treating $G$ as a parameter estimated from $\Omega$ given $(e, M)$ and inverting the Cramér-Rao lower bound $\mathrm{Var}(\hat{G}) \geq \mathbf{I}_G^{-1}$ to obtain an upper bound on $\Vert\Delta M_{\text{bias}}\Vert$ goes in the wrong direction. Cramér-Rao bounds estimator error from below, not above; inverting it for an upper bound on side-information-induced displacement requires a fixed-estimator-class assumption that is not available in coupled-architecture settings.

**(F2) Rate-distortion inversion fails.** Applying Shannon's rate-distortion inequality and inverting to bound the maximum displacement induced by side-information injection conflates source-coding (representations of a source) with side-information injection (perturbation of an update). The structure does not invert.

The pattern across (F1) and (F2): information-theoretic *lower-bound* machinery for estimator or encoder performance does not invert to upper bounds on side-information-induced displacement. Upper bounds on displacement require *transport-inequality* machinery (Pinsker, Otto-Villani, Bakry-Émery, Lipschitz-posterior). The successful routes — Track 1 via Otto-Villani + Stuart, Track 2 via Čencov + Amari-Nagaoka — sit on this side of the divide.

### Relation to existing decompositions ^sec-discussion-decompositions

\citet{zeng-etal-2026}'s HalluGuard framework decomposes hallucination risk into *data-driven* and *reasoning-driven* components, linked respectively to training-time mismatches and inference-time instabilities. This is the closest-shape decomposition to our $\kappa_{\text{processing}} \times I$ factorization. The structural axes differ: HalluGuard's axis is *temporal* (training-time vs inference-time errors); ours is *architectural-vs-informational* (where the goal can flow vs how much it could resolve). The two decompositions live at different layers and do not reduce to each other. A model can have low data-driven hallucination (clean training) and still exhibit large goal-coupling displacement (Coupled-class architecture in a high-ambiguity regime), and vice versa.

\citet{chlon-karim-chlon-2025} frame transformers as *Bayesian in expectation but not in realization*, with permutation-induced order effects scaling as $O(\log n)$. This is structurally distinct from our framing — we treat the architecture as performing a single goal-conditioned belief update on a fixed input, not as averaging over orderings — but the two are compatible: their Quantified Martingale Violation bounds the deviation from Bayesian-in-realization behavior, and our bound captures one specific channel (goal-coupling) of how non-Bayesian-in-realization a coupled architecture can be.
