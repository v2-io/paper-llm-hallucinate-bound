## Introduction ^sec-introduction

Two literatures have grown up in parallel around what is, at root, a single question: when a goal-conditioned probabilistic model — a large language model interpreting an ambiguous prompt, an amortized Bayesian inference network producing a posterior, a policy-conditioned belief-update rule — produces an output whose post-update model state has been pulled away from its goal-marginal operating baseline by the goal channel, how far can the goal pull it before the displacement becomes a theorem rather than an anecdote? (The bound's formal baseline is the goal-marginal; under an additional architectural commitment (H$_{\text{neutral}}$) — automatic for Class 1 (Separated) — the bound also controls displacement from an evidence-only, goal-blind baseline; [[#^sec-bias-baseline]] makes this precise.)

The first literature targets the phenomenon. A sequence of recent results — Kalai and Vempala's *Calibrated Language Models Must Hallucinate* \cite{kalai-vempala-2023}; Kalai, Nachum, Vempala, and Zhang's *Why Language Models Hallucinate* \cite{kalai-nachum-vempala-zhang-2026}; Karbasi, Montasser, Sous, and Velegkas on the (im)possibility of automated hallucination detection \cite{karbasi-montasser-sous-velegkas-2025}; Wu, Grama, and Szpankowski's VC-dimension impossibility \cite{wu-grama-szpankowski-2024}; Suzuki, He, Tian, and Wang's probabilistic-negligibility positive result \cite{suzuki-he-tian-wang-2025}; Guo and Li's rate-distortion theorem for membership testing \cite{guo-li-2026}; Liu, Hu, Zhang, Song, and Liu's structural-misalignment formalization \cite{liu-hu-zhang-song-liu-2025}; Chlon, Karim, and Chlon's predictable-compression-failures account \cite{chlon-karim-chlon-2025}; and Zeng et al.'s data-vs-reasoning decomposition \cite{zeng-etal-2026} — has produced impressively varied lower bounds on hallucination *frequency*: how often a calibrated model must produce false output as a consequence of training-data structure, calibration constraints, learnability bottlenecks, computability obstructions, or evaluation incentives. None of these results engages the *architecture's belief-goal coupling structure* directly. Hallucination is treated as architecture-agnostic — an obstruction at the level of training-set statistics, classification capacity, or computability.

The second literature has the machinery but not the target. Bayesian inverse-problems theory in the lineage of Stuart's *Acta Numerica* article \cite{stuart-2010} has spent fifteen years building tight Lipschitz-stability bounds on the posterior under perturbations of the prior, the likelihood, and the data — Sprungk's local-Lipschitz extension \cite{sprungk-2020}; Cvetković and Lie's matched upper-and-lower bounds \cite{cvetkovic-lie-2025}; Dolera and Mainini's Fisher-information-flavored constants in the optimal-transport framework \cite{dolera-mainini-2023}; Garbuno-Iñigo, Helin, Hoffmann, and Hosseini's integral-probability-metric perturbation analysis \cite{garbuno-inigo-etal-2023}; Latz's well-posedness with weaker continuity \cite{latz-2020}; and Hosseini, Hsu, and Taghvaei's conditional optimal transport for amortized Bayesian inference \cite{hosseini-hsu-taghvaei-2025}. The transport-inequality apparatus that drives these bounds — Otto and Villani's $W_2$-vs-KL inequality under log-Sobolev concentration \cite{otto-villani-2000}, Bakry and Émery's curvature-dimension condition \cite{bakry-emery-1985}, the Wasserstein dynamics of Dolera, Favaro, and Mainini \cite{dolera-favaro-mainini-2024}, the Sinkhorn/Schrödinger contraction of Del Moral \cite{del-moral-2026} — is mature, well-understood, and verified across decades of inverse-problems practice. None of it has been brought to bear on belief-goal-coupled inference.

This paper composes the two. We bound a *displacement* — the goal-conditional bias in the post-update model state — by the *transferred goal-information* that enters the post-update model. The umbrella theorem is

$$\mathbb{E}\,\|\Delta M_{\text{bias}}\| \;\leq\; C \cdot \sqrt{\,I(G;\,M_{\tau^+} \mid e_\tau,\, M_{\tau^-})\,},$$

with $C$ supplied by either of two named tracks under regularity hypotheses on the post-update distribution. Under an additional architectural-bandwidth hypothesis (H$_\kappa$) — automatic by data processing for Separated-class architectures, a named structural commitment for non-Separated ones — the bound factors into an *architectural coupling factor* $\kappa_{\text{processing}}$ (the ratio of transferred to in-principle resolvable goal-information; typically $\leq 1$ for natural architectures, by data-processing on the architectural channel) and a *residual ambiguity factor* $I(G;\Omega \mid e, M)$ (how much the goal could in principle resolve about the latent world-state beyond what the evidence already pins down):

$$\mathbb{E}\,\|\Delta M_{\text{bias}}\| \;\leq\; C \cdot \sqrt{\,\kappa_{\text{processing}} \cdot I(G;\,\Omega \mid e,\, M)\,}.$$

The square-root scaling is a feature of the underlying transport-inequality machinery, not a presentational choice: information-theoretic upper bounds on metric displacement under log-Sobolev concentration or under the Fisher-Rao second-order expansion of KL share this $\sqrt{I}$ form. Track 1 additionally delivers the tighter *squared-distance, linear-in-$I$* form $\mathbb{E}\,\|\Delta M_{\text{bias}}\|^2 \leq C^2 \cdot I$ on the post-update law; Jensen's inequality then yields the distance form above. The unconditional theorem is the structural backbone — verifiable on its own terms without committing to any architectural hypothesis — while the architectural corollary is what gives the bound its operational reading as a κ × 𝒜 factorization.

The contribution is fourfold and is driven by what the no-go forces.

**(1) An architectural classification.** Whether the bias bound has a meaningful $\kappa_{\text{processing}}$ at all depends on a structural fact about the model's processing topology: whether the goal is causally upstream of the belief-update mechanism. [[#^sec-setup]] partitions architectures by their *Goal/Update Coupling Class* (or just *Class*) into three monotonically ordered cases — Class 1 (Separated), Class 2 (Partial), and Class 3 (Coupled) — and grounds the partition in the Pearl-blanket reading of the Markov-blanket apparatus distinguished by Bruineberg, Dolega, Dewhurst, and Baltieri \cite{bruineberg-etal-2022}. Plain decoder-only transformer attention is Class 3 (Coupled) by construction in a precise structural sense ([[#^thm-attention-coupled]]: every internal computation has a directed-graph path from any input position whenever attention weights are non-degenerate, by induction on layer depth) — so the goal is causally upstream of every quantity contributing to a post-update model state under any of the three natural readings (output distribution, KV-cache, appended context). The bias-bound *application* additionally invokes the in-context-learning correspondence between the next-token distribution and an implicit posterior \cite{garg-etal-2022,akyurek-etal-2023,vonoswald-etal-2023,xie-etal-2022}; [[#^sec-attention-coupled]] makes the connection precise. LLM systems with retrieval, tools, scratchpads, or external memory mix modular pathways at the system level and are appropriately Class 2 (Partial).

**(2) A transport-inequality track for the constant $C$.** Under two regularity hypotheses (statistical-manifold sub-case, Talagrand $T_2$ inequality on the goal-marginal post-update distribution), [[#^sec-track1]] derives the *unconditional* squared-distance bound $\mathbb{E}\,W_2^2 \leq C_{T_2} \cdot I(G; M_{\tau^+} \mid e, M_{\tau^-})$ on the post-update law, and the corresponding distance bound $\mathbb{E}\,W_2 \leq \sqrt{C_{T_2}}\sqrt{I(G; M_{\tau^+} \mid e, M_{\tau^-})}$ by Jensen. The cascade is two steps — chain rule of relative entropy on the post-update law, then Talagrand $T_2$ slice-wise — and never leaves the model space. Sufficient conditions for ($T_2$) include LSI on the post-update distribution (via \citet{otto-villani-2000}, recovering the canonical $C_{T_2} = 2 L_{\text{post}}^2/\rho_{\text{LSI}}$) and dimension-free sub-Gaussian Lipschitz concentration \cite{bobkov-gotze-1999,gozlan-2009}. Bounded support alone delivers only the weaker $T_1$ form ([[#^sec-h2-prime-suff]]) — adequate for the distance reading. Under the architectural-bandwidth hypothesis (H$_\kappa$), [[#^cor-track1-arch]] recovers the architectural factorization $\mathbb{E}\,W_2 \leq \sqrt{C_{T_2} \cdot \kappa_{\text{processing}} \cdot I(G;\Omega \mid e, M)}$. The closest single existing analog is Hosseini-Hsu-Taghvaei's conditional optimal transport for amortized inference, which we cite-and-distinguish: related machinery, different application.

**(3) A no-go theorem that forces a coordinate commitment.** [[#^sec-no-go]] exhibits a scale-family counterexample showing that *no coordinate-independent universal $C$* exists for Euclidean chart norms — i.e., no constant valid uniformly across charts on a given statistical-manifold sub-case. (A specific chart, such as the $\log\sigma$ chart on the Gaussian scale family, can keep the displacement bounded; what cannot exist is a single $C$ that works under *every* Euclidean chart while the right-hand side stays chart-invariant.) The counterexample is short and counterexample-grade. The no-go is *adjacent in shape* to Owhadi, Scovel, and Sullivan's Bayesian-brittleness results \cite{owhadi-scovel-sullivan-2015a,owhadi-scovel-sullivan-2015b} but is *not the same theorem*: their no-go is for arbitrary stability under finite information; ours is for a coordinate-independent universal $C$ absent a coordinate-invariance commitment. The [[#^sec-track1]] hypotheses explicitly exclude the brittleness regime.

**(4) A Fisher-Rao track that lifts the no-go — at two universal constants.** [[#^sec-track2]] introduces the parameterization-invariance axiom (PI) — read at full Markov-morphism strength — and shows that under the triple **(PI)** + **(R)** Riemannian structure on $\mathcal{M}$ + **(K)** KL as the bound's information coordinate, Čencov's 1982 uniqueness theorem \cite{cencov-1982} forces the Fisher information metric and pins the constant $C_{FR} = \sqrt{2}$ universally in the uniform-locality regime ([[#^thm-fr-uniqueness]], locally tight under (H4$'$)). The unconditional Track 2 bound ([[#^thm-track2-uncond]]) is $\mathbb{E}\,d_{FR} \leq \sqrt{2}\,\sqrt{I(G; M_{\tau^+} \mid e, M_{\tau^-})}\,(1+o(1))$. Outside the uniform-locality regime — when rare goals carry disproportionately large transferred information, or the operating distribution sits at moderate-to-large $I$ — a *globally valid* Fisher-Rao bound is available under strictly weaker hypotheses ([[#^thm-track2-glob]]: drop (R), (K), and (H4$'$); keep only (PI)): $\mathbb{E}\,d_{FR} \leq (\pi/\sqrt 2)\,\sqrt{I(G; M_{\tau^+} \mid e, M_{\tau^-})}$. The $\pi/2 \approx 1.57$ overhead is the worst-case arc-chord ratio at the unit $L^2$-sphere's antipode — proved via a global chord-arc bound $d_{FR} \le \pi\,\mathrm{Hel}$ ([[#^lem-chord-arc]]) combined with Tsybakov's $2\,\mathrm{Hel}^2 \le \mathrm{KL}$. A direct Hellinger statement ([[#^thm-hellinger]]) gives the chord companion at universal $1/\sqrt 2$. Under (H$_\kappa$), all three corollaries recover the architectural factorization. Lebanon's extended Čencov-Campbell characterization for conditional information geometry \cite{lebanon-2004} grounds the move in the natural conditional setting.

**The two-strand fragmentation, the no-go that bridges them, and the coordinate commitment that lifts the bound to a universal constant** are the paper's structural argument. We do not claim the bound is *the* explanation of LLM hallucination — we claim it is the bias-bound theorem the architecture-engaged geometric route delivers, and that the existing statistical-inevitability and capacity-impossibility lineages address an orthogonal axis.

The paper is theory-only. We do not instrument an LLM's belief-update dynamics for empirical validation — that is genuine future work. The mitigation we offer is structural: the Track 1 cascade has decades of empirical use in Bayesian inverse problems; the Track 2 bound is mathematically airtight under (PI) and Čencov; the no-go is counterexample-grade. [[#^sec-limitations]] spells out the limitations honestly.
